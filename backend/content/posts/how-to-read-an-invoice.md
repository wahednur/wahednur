An invoice looks busy the first time. Each part answers one question.

1. **Invoice number** (for example `INV-2026-0001`): the reference to quote in any message about this bill.
2. **Issue date and due date**: when it was sent and when payment is expected.
3. **Bill to**: the client name on the record. Tell me if it needs correcting, since it is used for your accounts.
4. **Lines**: each piece of work with quantity, unit price and amount.
5. **Discount**: shown as its own line, never mixed into prices.
6. **Total**: the full amount in one currency.
7. **Installments**: how the total is split, with a due date for each.
8. **Paid and balance**: what has been received so far and what is still due.
9. **Payment instructions**: how to pay and what to quote so the payment is matched correctly.

## Status words

- **Draft**: still being prepared; not yet sent.
- **Issued**: final. Lines can no longer be changed.
- **Partly paid**: some money is received, a balance remains.
- **Paid**: the balance is zero.
- **Overdue**: an installment passed its due date without payment.

## If something looks wrong

Tell me the invoice number and the line. Because issued invoices are frozen, a correction is made openly, not by quietly editing numbers.
