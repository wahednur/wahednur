Packages are fixed offers with a stated price, such as Basic, Standard and Premium for a service.

## The steps

1. **Choose a package** on the packages page. The price you see is the price recorded for your order. Later price changes do not alter it.
2. **Sign in** if you have not. You come back to the same package afterwards.
3. **Send the request.** I am notified. You can cancel it while it is waiting.
4. **I accept or decline.** Accepting a one-time package creates a project and a draft quotation for the agreed price.
5. **Quotation, then invoice.** You accept the quotation, it becomes an invoice, and payments are recorded against it.

## Monthly and yearly packages

For ongoing packages, such as maintenance, accepting starts a subscription. One invoice is created per period, never twice for the same period. You can see it in your account, and either of us can cancel.

## What stays under your control

Nothing is charged automatically behind your back. Every invoice is visible in your account, and you can see exactly what is due and when.
