AI can write confident text about anything, including things that are false. For business software that is a risk, so I set rules.

## Rules I follow

1. **Facts come from the database, not from the model.** Prices, stock and warranty are shown straight from stored data. A model is never asked to remember them.
2. **Output is checked.** On this site, automatic search-engine titles and descriptions are generated from the page's own words. If the text contains a number that is not in the page, it is rejected.
3. **There is always a fallback.** If the AI service fails, a plain rule-based generator writes the text, so the page is never left without one.
4. **Hand-written beats generated.** If I write the SEO text myself, the system locks it and never overwrites it.
5. **No fake urgency.** I do not use AI or anything else to invent countdowns or "only 2 left" messages.

## What I do not offer

I do not promise that AI will grow your sales or replace your staff. I build features that are grounded in your own data and can be checked.
